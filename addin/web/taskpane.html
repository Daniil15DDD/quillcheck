QuillCheck

Model estimates only, not evidence of who wrote a text. Runs on your Mac (localhost). Not comparable with GPTZero or Turnitin.

Sentences to reread

Yellow marks sentences only inside paragraphs the classifier rates 50%+ likely AI-generated, and within those the more machine-like half. Nothing is marked when the whole text leans human-written. Single-sentence scores are noisy, so a mark is a reason to reread, not a verdict.

AI-assisted editing: review every change. A small local model writes the suggestions and can change meaning. It is not designed or tested to change detector results. Follow your university's rules on AI use.
